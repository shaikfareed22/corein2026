# FOMO 1.2.1 · Clear

**FOMO `1.2.1` “Clear”** asks for your e-mail and speaks English.

The sign-in screen on every device says *Enter your email*. The Chinese translations, the Chinese-only prompts and every mention of a region are gone from the apps, the relay's console and the sign-in e-mail. Nothing about how the agent works changed.

## Highlights

- **Sign in with your e-mail.** The field says so on Android, iPhone, the desktop and the web console, and a phone number gets one sentence: codes are sent by e-mail only.
- **One language family less.** The app no longer ships a Chinese translation or Chinese prompts; a phone set to Chinese shows FOMO in English.
- **The code e-mail** is English only and arrives from the sender name your relay sets (`corein` when none is set).

## Upgrade Notes

- **Android installs over 1.2.0 and keeps your data.** `fomo-1.2.1-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0+, arm64; versionCode 51) is signed with the same key as 1.2.0. Verify with `sha256sum -c fomo-1.2.1-arm64.apk.sha256`.
- **The desktop app installs over 1.2.0.** The installers are not notarised: on macOS open the app once from *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*; on Windows click *Run anyway*. They arrive a little after the APK, and `SHA256SUMS-desktop.txt` lists every checksum.
- **A self-hosted relay** gets the new e-mail and console with `git pull` and `docker compose up -d --build`.
- **Where to get it.**
  | | |
  |---|---|
  | Android 8.0+, arm64 | `fomo-1.2.1-arm64.apk` |
  | Windows 10+ | `fomo-Desktop-1.2.1-win-x64.exe` |
  | macOS 12+ | `fomo-Desktop-1.2.1-mac-arm64.dmg` / `-mac-x64.dmg` |
  | Linux x64 | `fomo-Desktop-1.2.1-linux-x64.deb` / `.AppImage` / `.tar.gz` (run `./fomo-desktop`) |
